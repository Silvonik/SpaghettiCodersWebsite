What Is Spaghetti Coders?

We are a club located in Dublin, Ireland for teens from the age of 13-18, we make games, websites, electronics, 3D prints, and many more projects using the workshops Hack Club provides

FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions with answers!

Who can join Spaghetti Coders?

Anyone from the ages of 13-18 can join! Doesn't matter if you're a total beginner or a full pro if you're interested in making websites, games, electronics, 3D printing and just having a good time, thats all you need to join!

Is Spaghetti Coders free to join?

Yes, 100% free!

When do we meet?

We host weekly meetings every week on Saturday at _:00 PM.

Where do we meet?

We host meetings at Coolock Library in Dublin 17.

What do I need to bring?

Bring a Laptop, a charger for your laptop (Just in case), and yourself!
