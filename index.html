What Is Spaghetti Coders?

We are a technology/coding club led by a teen located in Dublin, Ireland for teens aged 13-18, we make games, websites, electronics, 3D prints, and whatever else we come up with!

We host weekly meetings where we meet up, build projects, present our projects, and have fun!

We are supported by Hackclub, a nonprofit that helps teens aged 13-18 build coding/technology projects.

FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions with answers!

Who can join Spaghetti Coders?

Anyone aged 13-18 can join! It doesn't matter if you're a total beginner or an experienced coder. If you're interested in making games, websites, electronics, 3D prints, or just making cool stuff, you're welcome!

How do I join Spaghetti Coders?

Click the Join button in the Join Us section and log into the Clubs Dashboard, please use your personal email and make sure the information you provide is accurate when logging in. Then come along to one of our weekly meetings and let us know your name so we can find your join request. Once we confirm it's you, we'll approve your request and you'll officially become a Spaghetti Coder!

How much does it cost?

Nothing! Spaghetti Coders is completely free to join.

When and where do you meet?

We meet every Saturday from 3:15-4:45 PM at Coolock Library in Dublin 17.

What do we do at the meetings?

At our meetings, we meet up, build projects, eat snacks, present what we've made, and have fun! We might make games, websites, electronics, 3D prints, or take part in workshops provided by Hack Club.

Do I need to know how to code?

Nope! Beginners are completely welcome. You don't need to know how to code or have any previous experience.

What do I need to bring?

Bring a laptop and its charger.

Do I have to come to every meeting?

No. You can come along whenever you're available. We also have a Discord community where members can keep up with announcements and share their projects between meetings.
